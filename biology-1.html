Practical laboratory

Core Practicals 01–04.

Practical knowledge is part of the specification. These investigations connect theory with experimental design, observations, data collection and analysis.

CORE PRACTICAL 01

Reducing sugars & starch

Use Benedict's reagent to estimate reducing sugar concentration and iodine solution to estimate starch concentration using colour standards.

CORE PRACTICAL 02

Vitamin C in food & drink

Investigate vitamin C content and use quantitative observations to compare food and drink samples.

CORE PRACTICAL 03

Membrane permeability

Investigate membrane properties and examine the effects of alcohol and temperature on membrane permeability.

CORE PRACTICAL 04

Enzyme-catalysed reactions

Investigate how temperature, pH, enzyme concentration and substrate concentration affect the initial rate of enzyme reactions.

IAL ATLAS strategy

How to master Unit 1.

Don't just memorise isolated facts. Build connections between molecules, structures, mechanisms, evidence and biological consequences.

🧬

Learn structures

Draw and recognise carbohydrates, triglycerides, proteins, nucleotides, membranes and DNA. Structure-function relationships are central.

❤️

Follow mechanisms

Practise explaining processes step-by-step: cardiac cycle, clotting, atherosclerosis, membrane transport, replication and protein synthesis.

📊

Analyse evidence

Get comfortable interpreting graphs, tables, correlations, experimental results and studies of health risks.
