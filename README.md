## 📚 About IAL ATLAS

**IAL ATLAS** is a free, student-built educational platform created to make **Pearson Edexcel International A Level** learning easier, clearer, and more accessible.

The platform brings together structured notes, revision material, practical guidance, exam-focused resources, formulas, explanations, and study materials in one organized place.

Instead of making students search through scattered resources, IAL ATLAS aims to create a simple digital environment where students can navigate their subjects and units efficiently.

> **Built by a student. Made for students. Free to learn from.**

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Studying for International A Levels can involve a huge amount of information spread across textbooks, notes, websites, videos, practical resources, and past-paper material.

IAL ATLAS was created with a simple idea:

### Make learning easier.

The goal is to provide students with a structured platform where difficult topics can be explored step by step without unnecessary complexity.
