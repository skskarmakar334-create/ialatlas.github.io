MECHANICS 2 • TOPIC 03

Work and Energy

Understand work done by forces and use energy methods to solve mechanics problems.

LESSON 01

What is Work?

Work is done when a force causes a displacement in the direction of the force.

Key idea

Work is a measure of the energy transferred by a force.

Work done = Force × displacement

LESSON 02

Work Done by a Constant Force

If a constant force F acts in the same direction as the displacement s, then:

W = Fs

Work is measured in joules (J).

LESSON 03

Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by a particle because of its motion.

KE = ½mv²

Here m is the mass and v is the speed of the particle.

LESSON 04

Gravitational Potential Energy

When a particle is raised through a vertical height h, its gravitational potential energy increases.

GPE = mgh

The value of g is the acceleration due to gravity.

LESSON 05

Work-Energy Principle

The work done by the resultant force on a particle is equal to its change in kinetic energy.

Work done = Change in kinetic energy

Therefore:

W = ½mv² − ½mu²

where u is the initial speed and v is the final speed.

LESSON 06

Conservation of Energy

Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only be transferred from one form to another.

For mechanics problems:

Initial energy = Final energy

KE + GPE = constant

WORKED EXAMPLE

Work Done by a Force

Example

A constant force of 12 N moves a particle through 5 m in the direction of the force. Find the work done.

W = Fs

W = 12 × 5

W = 60 J

WORKED EXAMPLE

Kinetic Energy

Example

A particle of mass 4 kg moves with speed 6 m/s. Find its kinetic energy.

KE = ½mv²

KE = ½ × 4 × 6²

KE = 2 × 36

KE = 72 J

PRACTICE

Test Yourself

Show answer

Show answer

KE = ½ × 5 × 4²

KE = 40 J

3. What is the gravitational potential energy of a mass m raised through height h?
Show answer

GPE = mgh
