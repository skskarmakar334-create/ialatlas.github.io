MECHANICS 2 • TOPIC 05

Statics of Rigid Bodies

Learn how forces and moments act on rigid bodies and how equilibrium can be analysed.

LESSON 01

What is a Rigid Body?

A rigid body is an object whose shape and size are assumed not to change when forces act on it.

In mechanics:

A rigid body can experience forces and turning effects, but its dimensions remain fixed.

LESSON 02

Resultant Force

For a rigid body to remain in equilibrium, the vector sum of all forces acting on it must be zero.

Resultant force = 0

This means that the horizontal and vertical components of the forces must balance.

LESSON 03

Moments

A force can cause a rigid body to rotate about a point. The turning effect of a force is called its moment.

Moment = Force × Perpendicular distance

The unit of moment is newton-metres (N m).

LESSON 04

Equilibrium of a Rigid Body

A rigid body is in equilibrium when there is no translational or rotational acceleration.

Two important conditions:
  • The resultant force is zero.
  • The resultant moment about any point is zero.
Sum of clockwise moments = Sum of anticlockwise moments

LESSON 05

Taking Moments About a Point

Choosing a suitable point about which to take moments can make a problem much easier.

Forces whose lines of action pass through the chosen point have zero moment about that point.

Useful strategy:

Choose a point that eliminates one or more unknown forces from the moment equation.

LESSON 06

Centre of Mass and Weight

The weight of a rigid body acts vertically downward through its centre of mass.

Weight = mg

When analysing moments, the perpendicular distance from the centre of mass to the chosen point must be used.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Balancing Moments

Example

A force of 10 N acts at a perpendicular distance of 2 m from a pivot. What force acting at a perpendicular distance of 4 m would balance it?

Clockwise moment = Anticlockwise moment

10 × 2 = F × 4

20 = 4F

F = 5 N

PRACTICE

Test Yourself

1. What are the two conditions required for a rigid body to be in equilibrium?
Show answer

The resultant force must be zero and the resultant moment must be zero.

2. Find the moment of a 15 N force acting at a perpendicular distance of 3 m from a pivot.
Show answer

Moment = 15 × 3

Moment = 45 N m

3. A 20 N force acts 2 m from a pivot. What force acting 5 m from the pivot would balance it?
Show answer

20 × 2 = F × 5

40 = 5F

F = 8 N
