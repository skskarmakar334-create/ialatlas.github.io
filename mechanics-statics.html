MECHANICS 1 • TOPIC 05

Statics of a Particle

Learn how forces balance when a particle is in equilibrium.

LESSON 01

What is Statics?

Statics is the study of objects that are in equilibrium.

Key idea

A particle is in equilibrium when the resultant force acting on it is zero.

LESSON 02

Equilibrium

For a particle to remain in equilibrium, the forces acting on it must balance.

Resultant force = 0

This means there is no acceleration.

LESSON 03

Resolving Forces

A force acting at an angle can be resolved into horizontal and vertical components.

Horizontal component = F cos θ
Vertical component = F sin θ

These components can then be used to determine whether the particle is in equilibrium.

LESSON 04

Equilibrium Equations

For equilibrium in two dimensions, the horizontal and vertical forces must each balance.

ΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0

These equations are used to find unknown forces or angles.

LESSON 05

Weight and Tension

Two common forces in mechanics problems are weight and tension.

Weight

W = mg and acts vertically downward.

Tension

Tension is a pulling force transmitted through a string or cable.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Simple Equilibrium

Example

A particle is acted on by two horizontal forces: 15 N to the right and an unknown force to the left. The particle is in equilibrium. Find the unknown force.

Since the particle is in equilibrium:

ΣFx = 0

15 − F = 0

F = 15

F = 15 N
