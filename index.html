THE PERSON BEHIND IAL ATLAS

Meet Shuvojit.

Founder & Creator of IAL ATLAS

“I created IAL ATLAS with a simple belief: learning should be easier to access, easier to understand, and never feel like something you have to figure out alone.”

As a student, I know how overwhelming International A Levels can be. There are countless topics to learn, formulas to remember, questions to practise and exams to prepare for. Sometimes, what a student needs isn't more information — it's a clearer way of understanding it.

That's why I created IAL ATLAS — an independent, student-built platform designed to bring clear explanations, worked examples, practice questions and revision resources together in one place.

This isn't just a website. It's a project built to help students.

Built for students, by a student.
— Shuvojit Karmakar
